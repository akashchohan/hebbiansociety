I

The Idea

One neuron can do almost nothing. Connect the right ones, and you get a mind.

The Hebbian Society is a private circle of people from completely different fields: artificial intelligence, medicine, engineering, business, and beyond.

We learn each other's worlds. We take on problems that no single field can solve alone. And from time to time, we release work that makes the world look twice.

II

On the Name

In 1949, the psychologist Donald Hebb proposed in The Organization of Behavior that when one brain cell repeatedly helps to fire another, the connection between them grows stronger. Learning, he argued, is connection strengthened by use.

The Society is built on the same principle. Minds from different fields, brought together and set to work on the same problems, until the connections between them become stronger than any one of them alone.

III

Principles

Curiosity First

We value how someone thinks over the titles they hold.

Every Field Matters

The answer often lives in the field nobody thought to ask.

Discretion

What is shared within the Society stays within it, until it is ready.

Work That Lasts

We would rather release one remarkable thing than many forgettable ones.

IV

Membership

By invitation. No one applies. Every invitation is a handwritten, numbered letter from the founder, delivered by hand.

The token. Every member holds a token unique to them. Keep it. One day it will be asked for.

The rounds. Members are tested in rounds, each harder than the last, and each asking for more than any single field can offer. Not everyone passes every round.
